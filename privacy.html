Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Dog Dad Boarding ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information you submit via our booking or newsletter forms (name, email, phone).
  • Details about your dog (breed, age, special needs) provided in booking requests.
  • Basic usage data (page views) via privacy-friendly analytics, if enabled.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to booking inquiries and confirm appointments.
  • To send newsletters and promotions you opted into (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • To improve our services.

We Do Not

  • Sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • Share your information except as required to fulfil services (e.g., payment processing).
