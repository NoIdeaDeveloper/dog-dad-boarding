Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

Dog Dad Boarding ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website dogdadboarding.com (the "Site"). We are a small, local dog boarding, daycare, grooming, and walking business based in Austin, Texas. We know that handing over personal information — and your dog's information — requires trust, and we take that trust seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect, why we collect it, where it goes, who can see it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have over it. We have written this to be thorough but also readable — you should not need a law degree to understand how your data is handled here.

By using our Site, submitting a booking request, or subscribing to our newsletter, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of it, please do not use the Site or submit information to us. You are always welcome to reach out with questions before sharing anything — see Section 13.

1. Information We Collect

Not all information is equal. Some you choose to give us; some your browser shares automatically when any website loads. We separate these because the rules around them — and your choices — are different.

Information you provide directly

This is information you enter into a form, type into an email, or tell us in person. You decide whether to share it, and most of it is optional.

  • Booking requests. When you fill out our "Ready to Book?" contact form, we collect the fields you submit: your name, your email address, your phone number (optional), your dog's name, the service you are interested in (overnight boarding, daycare, grooming, or dog walking), your requested start and end dates, and any details you choose to share in the "Tell Us About Your Dog" box — typically breed, age, vaccinations, special needs, temperament, and personality quirks. The more you share, the better we can care for your dog, but you are never required to fill in optional fields.
  • Newsletter subscriptions. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect only your email address. Nothing else.
  • Correspondence. If you email, text, or message us directly, we keep whatever information is in those messages, including attachments such as photos of your dog. This is necessary to provide the service you are asking us for.
  • Information you give us in person. When you drop off or pick up your dog, we may record notes about behavior, feeding, or health observations during the stay. These notes become part of your customer record.

Information collected automatically

This is information your device and browser share with every website you visit, not just ours. It is a normal part of how the internet works.

  • Server logs. When you load a page on our Site, our hosting provider may record standard request data: your IP address (a number that identifies your internet connection), browser type and version, the page you requested, the page you came from (the "referrer"), and the date and time. We use this only to keep the Site running, diagnose technical problems, and understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, which pages are most visited. We do not use it to build a profile of you as an individual.
  • Local storage. We do not set tracking cookies. Your browser may use its built-in local storage to remember small things, such as a half-completed form if you navigate away and return. This data lives on your device, not on our servers, and you can clear it from your browser settings at any time.

Information we do not collect

It is just as important to be clear about what we do not do:

  • We do not collect payment information through our Site. Any payments are handled separately and securely when we confirm a booking with you directly.
  • We do not collect precise location data from your device.
  • We do not run background tracking that follows you across other websites or apps.

2. How We Use Your Information

Every piece of information we collect has a specific, limited purpose. We do not collect data "just in case" or to build profiles for advertising.

  • To respond to your booking inquiry. When you submit a booking request, we use your name and contact details to reply to you, confirm availability, ask follow-up questions, and send pre-arrival information. We use your dog's details to prepare appropriately for their stay — for example, making sure we have the right food, bedding, or accommodations ready.
  • To communicate during a stay. While your dog is with us, we send daily photo and video updates as described in our services. We may also contact you if anything comes up — a change in appetite, a minor health concern, or simply a great play session we thought you'd enjoy seeing.
  • To send newsletters and offers. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we use your email address to send updates, seasonal promotions, and dog-care tips. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any newsletter email, or by contacting us. We will never send marketing emails to someone who has not opted in.
  • To maintain care records. We keep notes about your dog's stay — behavior, feeding, health observations, and anything that helps us provide consistent, personalized care next time. This is part of providing a service you have requested.
  • To improve our Site and services. We use aggregate, de-identified traffic data to understand which pages are useful and where visitors get stuck. This helps us make the Site better for everyone.
  • To keep the Site secure. We use server logs to detect and block abuse, such as repeated failed submissions or suspicious traffic patterns.
  • To comply with legal obligations. If we are legally required to retain or disclose information — for tax records, a court order, or to respond to a lawful request — we will do so to the minimum extent necessary.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you specific rights and requires us to have a valid legal basis for processing your personal information. We rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)). When you submit a booking request, subscribe to our newsletter, or send us a message, you are consenting to our use of that information for the purposes described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at hello@dogdadboarding.com. Withdrawing consent will not affect any processing that already took place before you withdrew.
  • Contract (Article 6(1)(b)). When you request a service from us, we need to process your information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract (confirming availability, preparing for your dog's stay) and to perform the contract once it is in place (caring for your dog, sending updates).
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)). We process limited data — such as server logs — to keep the Site secure, prevent abuse, and improve our services. We balance these interests against your rights and only process what is necessary.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). We may retain certain records, such as tax-related booking records, to comply with legal requirements.

If you have questions about the legal basis we rely on for a specific use of your data, please ask us — we are happy to explain.

4. Third-Party Services

We are a small business, and like most websites, we rely on a few trusted third-party services to keep things running. We have chosen services that align with our privacy values where possible. Each provider has its own privacy policy — we encourage you to read them if you want the full picture.

  • Form submission service. Our booking and newsletter forms are transmitted through a third-party form handler (the service named in the form's action attribute). When you click "Send Booking Request" or "Subscribe," your submission is sent to us through this service. We do not authorize the provider to use your data for their own marketing or analytics beyond what is needed to operate the service. The provider may retain temporary logs for abuse prevention.
  • Map provider — OpenStreetMap via Leaflet. Our "Serving All of Austin" map uses the Leaflet library to display OpenStreetMap tiles. When the map loads, OpenStreetMap's tile servers receive your IP address and a request for the specific map image you are viewing. This is how all map services work. OpenStreetMap does not track you across other sites. You can read their privacy policy at openstreetmap.org/privacy. To protect your privacy, our Site only loads the map when you scroll near it, rather than on every page load.
  • Font provider — Bunny Fonts. Our Site's typography is served by Bunny Fonts, a privacy-focused font CDN. Unlike some font services, Bunny Fonts does not set tracking cookies or build user profiles. You can read more at fonts.bunny.net/privacy.
  • Email provider. When we send newsletter emails, we use a third-party email service. The provider processes your email address and basic delivery data (such as whether an email bounced or was opened) strictly to deliver emails on our behalf. We configure the provider to avoid tracking pixels where possible.
  • Hosting provider. Our Site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider that stores the Site's files and serves them to visitors. The hosting provider may log basic request data (IP address, timestamps) for security and operational purposes.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party — not for advertising, not for analytics, not for any other purpose.

5. Cookies and Tracking

A "cookie" is a small text file a website stores in your browser. Cookies can be useful — for example, remembering that you are logged in — but they can also be used to track you across the web. Here is where we stand:

  • We do not set advertising cookies. No Google Ads, no Facebook Pixel, no retargeting tags.
  • We do not set third-party tracking cookies. No analytics cookies that follow you around the internet.
  • We do not embed social media "Like" or "Share" buttons that track you even when you do not click them.
  • We do not run cross-site tracking. Nothing on our Site is designed to identify you on other websites.

If we add privacy-friendly analytics in the future — for example, to understand which pages are most useful — it will be configured to anonymize IP addresses and not set identifying cookies. We will update this section before any such tool is added.

6. Data Sharing

We share your information only in limited, specific circumstances. Here is the complete list:

  • With our service providers (described in Section 4), solely to operate the Site and deliver services to you. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and contracts with us.
  • With veterinary partners, if your dog requires emergency care during a stay. In that situation, we will share only the information necessary to provide care — for example, your dog's name, age, breed, vaccination status, and any known health conditions. We will also notify you immediately, as described in our services.
  • When required by law. If we receive a valid court order, subpoena, or other lawful request, we may be legally obligated to disclose information. We will only disclose what is specifically required and nothing more.
  • To protect rights and safety. In rare cases — such as preventing fraud, protecting the safety of our staff or animals in our care, or enforcing our terms — we may share information as reasonably necessary.

We never share your information for marketing purposes, and we never sell it.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as we need it — either to provide our services, to meet legal obligations, or to respond to issues that may come up later. Here is how long different types of information are kept:

  • Booking records and related correspondence: up to 7 years. This period reflects typical tax and legal record-keeping requirements in Texas. After that, we delete or anonymize the records.
  • Dog care notes: kept as long as you are an active customer, then deleted or anonymized when you request it, or within a reasonable period after your last booking (typically 2 years).
  • Newsletter subscriber data: kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Unsubscribing is instant via the link in any newsletter email.
  • Server logs: kept for a short period (typically 30–90 days) for security and debugging, then automatically deleted.
  • Form submission provider logs: governed by the provider's own retention policy, typically short-term for abuse prevention.

You can request earlier deletion of your data at any time — see Section 8. We will honor that request unless we are legally required to retain the information.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you specific rights over your personal information. We honor these rights for everyone, regardless of where you are located, because we believe they are good practice.

  • Right of access. You can ask us what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy of it.
  • Right to correct. If information we hold about you or your dog is inaccurate or incomplete, you can ask us to fix it.
  • Right to delete (the "right to be forgotten"). You can ask us to delete your personal information, subject to legal retention obligations (for example, we may need to keep tax records).
  • Right to restrict processing. In certain circumstances, you can ask us to limit how we use your data — for example, while we verify a correction.
  • Right to object. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing. We will stop unless we have a compelling reason to continue.
  • Right to data portability. You can ask for a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format (such as a CSV or JSON file) so you can take it elsewhere.
  • Right to withdraw consent. If we are processing your data based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This will not affect processing that already took place.
  • Right to lodge a complaint. If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you can complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in California, you can complain to the California Attorney General. We would, of course, prefer you contact us first so we can try to resolve the issue directly.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@dogdadboarding.com with a clear description of your request. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask for information to verify your identity before acting on a request — this is to protect you from someone else trying to access or change your data.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) give you additional rights:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the business purpose for collecting it, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell or share your information for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of — but if this ever changes, we will provide a clear "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by California law (such as Social Security numbers, precise geolocation, or genetic data) through our Site.
  • Non-discrimination. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

California residents can submit requests to hello@dogdadboarding.com. We will verify your identity before responding.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults who are booking care for their pets. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Booking a service and caring for a dog require legal adulthood and responsibility, so there is no reason for a child to submit information to us.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information — for example, by filling out the contact form — please contact us at hello@dogdadboarding.com. We will delete the information and take steps to prevent it from happening again.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure. Here is what we actually do:

  • HTTPS encryption. All form submissions and page loads on our Site are encrypted in transit using HTTPS. This means someone watching your internet connection cannot easily read what you submit.
  • Limited access. Only the people who need access to customer records to do their jobs have it. Access is reviewed periodically.
  • Reputable providers. We use well-established third-party services that have their own security practices.
  • No payment data on our Site. Because we do not process payments through the Site, we never store credit card numbers or other financial account details.

If we become aware of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the appropriate authorities as required by law, as promptly as is reasonable in the circumstances.

12. International Transfers

Our Site is operated from the United States. If you access our Site or submit information from outside the US — for example, while traveling or from your home country — your information will be transferred to and processed in the US. The US may have data protection laws that differ from those in your country.

By using our Site and submitting information to us, you consent to that transfer. We handle your data in accordance with this policy no matter where you are located. If you are in a region with strong data protection laws (such as the EEA, UK, or Switzerland) and have concerns about international transfers, please contact us — we are happy to discuss the safeguards we rely on, such as standard contractual clauses where applicable.

  • For significant changes — such as a new category of data we collect or a new third-party service — we will provide a more prominent notice, such as a notice on our homepage or an email to newsletter subscribers.
  • For minor wording changes or clarifications, we will just update the date.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date tells you when anything last changed. If you want to be notified of significant changes, subscribing to our newsletter is the best way.
