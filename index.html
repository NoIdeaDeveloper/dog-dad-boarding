FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your pup's stay.

What vaccinations do you require?

We require proof of current Rabies, DHPP (Distemper/Parvo), and Bordetella (kennel cough) vaccinations. All vaccines must be administered at least 48 hours before check-in.

What should I bring for my dog's stay?

Bring your dog's regular food (pre-portioned), any medications, a leash/collar with ID tags, their bed or blanket if they have one, and favorite toys. We provide bowls, bedding, and enrichment toys.

Can I tour the facility before booking?

Absolutely! We encourage tours. Just reach out to schedule a visit. We'll show you our indoor/outdoor play areas, sleeping accommodations, and introduce you to the team.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 48 hours notice for cancellations to receive a full refund. Late cancellations within 24 hours are charged 50% of the booking. No-shows are charged in full.

Do you accept dogs that aren't spayed or neutered?

Yes, we accept intact dogs. However, they must be separated during group play sessions to ensure everyone's safety. We'll discuss the best arrangement during booking.

How do you handle emergencies?

We are pet first aid and CPR certified. We partner with Austin Emergency Veterinary Clinic for 24/7 support. You'll be notified immediately if any medical concern arises.
